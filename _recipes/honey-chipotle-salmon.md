## Ingredients
- 5 tablespoons (71g) unsalted butter, melted
- 1/4 cup (85g) honey
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground chipotle chili pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 4 salmon fillets (or one 2 lb fillet)

## Instructions
1. In a medium bowl, whisk 5 tablespoons melted butter, 1/4 cup honey, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1 teaspoon chipotle, 1/2 teaspoon pepper, and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
2. Put the salmon in a container, baking dish, or zip-top bag and pour half the marinade over it. Shake to coat. Reserve the other half. Marinate at least 15 minutes, up to 8 hours.
3. Heat the grill to medium-high, or preheat the oven to 375°F.
4. **To grill:** cook skin side down on foil or a cedar plank, about 10 minutes per inch of thickness (around 15 minutes), until the thickest part reaches 145°F.
   **To bake:** cook on a lined baking sheet 15-20 minutes, until the thickest part reaches 145°F.
5. Brush the warm salmon with the reserved half of the marinade before serving.

## Notes
- Leftovers keep a few days in the fridge.
