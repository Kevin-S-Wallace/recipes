## Ingredients
### Chicken marinade
- 28 oz (800g) boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 1 1/2 tablespoons minced garlic
- 1 tablespoon ginger, grated
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili (or 1/2 teaspoon ground red chili powder)
- 1 teaspoon salt

### Sauce
- 2 tablespoons vegetable or canola oil
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 small onions (or 1 large), finely diced
- 1 1/2 tablespoons garlic, finely grated
- 1 tablespoon ginger, finely grated
- 1 1/2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 14 oz (400g) tomato puree (passata)
- 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili (optional, for color and flavor)
- 1 teaspoon ground red chili powder, adjusted to taste
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/4 cups heavy cream (or evaporated milk for fewer calories)
- 1 teaspoon brown sugar
- 1/4 cup water, if needed
- 4 tablespoons fresh cilantro, to garnish

## Instructions
### Oven
1. In a bowl, combine the chicken with 1 cup yogurt, 1 1/2 tablespoons minced garlic, 1 tablespoon ginger, 2 teaspoons garam masala, 1 teaspoon turmeric, 1 teaspoon cumin, 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili, and 1 teaspoon salt. Marinate 10 minutes to 1 hour, or overnight.
2. Preheat the oven to 500°F. Line a high-sided baking pan with parchment paper.
3. Thread the chicken onto skewers and set them across the pan so there's space underneath. Bake about 15 minutes, until slightly dark brown on the edges.
4. In a pot, heat 2 tablespoons oil and melt 2 tablespoons butter. Fry the onions until soft, about 3 minutes, scraping up any browned bits.
5. Add 1 1/2 tablespoons garlic and 1 tablespoon ginger and sauté 1 minute until fragrant. Add 1 1/2 teaspoons garam masala, 1 1/2 teaspoons cumin, 1 teaspoon turmeric, and 1 teaspoon coriander and fry about 20 seconds, stirring.
6. Pour in 14 oz tomato puree, 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili, 1 teaspoon red chili powder, and 1 teaspoon salt. Simmer 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thick and deep red-brown.
7. Stir in 1 1/4 cups cream and 1 teaspoon brown sugar. Add the chicken and its juices and cook 8-10 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce is thick and bubbling. Thin with up to 1/4 cup water if needed.
8. Garnish with 4 tablespoons cilantro. Serve with [garlic butter rice](garlic-butter-rice.md) and [homemade naan](naan-bread.md).

### Slow cooker
1. Marinate the chicken as in oven step 1.
2. Optionally, soften the onions in a little oil in a pan first.
3. Add everything except the 1 1/4 cups cream and the cilantro to the slow cooker. Cook on low 6-8 hours.
4. About 30 minutes before serving, stir in 1 1/4 cups cream.
5. Cook 30 minutes more, then garnish with 4 tablespoons cilantro.
