## Ingredients
- 480g Kodiak pancake mix
- 3 cups milk
- 4 large eggs
- 1 pound breakfast sausage
- 2 cups blueberries
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F and grease a 9x13 baking dish.
2. Brown 1 pound breakfast sausage in a skillet, breaking it into crumbles. Drain.
3. In a large bowl, whisk 480g pancake mix, 3 cups milk, 4 eggs, 1 teaspoon vanilla, and 1 teaspoon cinnamon until combined.
4. Fold in the browned sausage and 2 cups blueberries.
5. Pour into the dish and bake 30-40 minutes, until the center is set.
