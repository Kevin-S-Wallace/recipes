## Ingredients
### Cookies
- 1 1/4 cups (282g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
- 1 cup (200g) packed dark brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg, at room temperature
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
- 1 tablespoon (21g) unsulphured or dark molasses (not blackstrap)
- 1 2/3 cups (209g) all-purpose flour, spooned and leveled
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
- 3 cups (255g) quick oats (not old-fashioned)

### Cream filling
- 3/4 cup (170g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
- 3 cups (360g) confectioners' sugar
- 3 tablespoons (45ml) heavy cream, at room temperature, plus more if needed
- 1 1/2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
- Salt, to taste

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats.
2. In a large bowl with a hand or stand mixer, beat 1 1/4 cups butter, 1 cup brown sugar, and 1/2 cup granulated sugar on medium-high until light and creamy, about 3 minutes, scraping down the bowl as needed.
3. Add 1 egg, 2 teaspoons vanilla, and 1 tablespoon molasses and beat on high until combined, about 1 minute. Scrape the bowl and beat again as needed.
4. In a medium bowl, whisk 1 2/3 cups flour, 1 teaspoon baking soda, 1/2 teaspoon salt, 3/4 teaspoon cinnamon, 1/4 teaspoon cloves, and 3 cups quick oats.
5. With the mixer on low, slowly add the dry ingredients to the wet. The dough will be very thick and sticky; finish mixing by hand if needed.
6. Scoop about 2 heaping tablespoons (45g) of dough per cookie and space them 3 inches apart, about 6 per sheet.
7. Bake 10-13 minutes, until very lightly golden around the edges. The centers will look very soft.
8. Cool on the sheet 10 minutes, then move to a rack to cool completely.
9. **Filling:** beat 3/4 cup butter on high until creamy, about 1 minute. Add 3 cups confectioners' sugar and beat on medium 1-2 minutes. Add 3 tablespoons heavy cream and 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla and beat on high 2-3 minutes. Taste and add a pinch or two of salt. If it's way too thick, beat in 1 more tablespoon heavy cream.
10. Spread about 1 1/2 tablespoons filling on the bottom of half the cookies and top with the rest, right side up.

## Notes
- Store covered at room temperature up to 2 days, or in the fridge up to 5 days.
- **Make ahead:** the dough keeps, tightly covered, in the fridge up to 3 days. Bring to room temperature before scooping (step 6). The cookies won't spread as much since the oats soak up moisture.
- **Freezing:** baked, unfilled cookies freeze up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge and bring to room temperature before filling (step 9); the filling is best made fresh. Unbaked dough balls also freeze up to 3 months; bake from frozen, adding a minute or two.
- Light brown sugar works in place of dark with no other changes.
- If you only have old-fashioned oats, pulse them about 10 times in a food processor first.
