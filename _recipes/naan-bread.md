## Ingredients
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 1 cup warm water
- 1/2 oz (2 packets, 4 1/2 teaspoons) active dry yeast
- 4 1/2 cups (563g) all-purpose flour, plus more if the dough is sticky
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 tablespoons oil, plus more for greasing the skillet
- 6 tablespoons butter, melted

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, stir together 2 teaspoons sugar, 1 cup warm water, and 4 1/2 teaspoons yeast. Let sit until foamy, about 10 minutes.
2. Pile 4 1/2 cups (563g) flour on a flat surface and make a well in the middle.
3. Add the yeast mixture, 1 cup yogurt, 2 teaspoons salt, and 2 tablespoons oil to the well. Knead until the dough is smooth and shiny, about 10 minutes. If it's sticky, knead in more flour a little at a time.
4. Cover with a damp cloth and let rise in a warm spot (next to the stove or a warm oven) until doubled, about 1 hour.
5. Divide the dough into 16 equal portions and roll each into an 8-inch circle.
6. Heat a skillet (cast iron preferred) over high heat and lightly grease it with oil.
7. Cook one naan at a time: when it puffs up, bubbles, and gets charred spots, flip and cook the other side. Re-grease the skillet as needed.
8. Brush the naan with 6 tablespoons melted butter and serve warm.

## Ingredients
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 1/4 oz (1 packet, 2 1/4 teaspoons) active dry yeast
- 2 1/4 cups (281g) all-purpose flour, plus more if the dough is sticky
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon oil, plus more for greasing the skillet
- 3 tablespoons butter, melted

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, stir together 1 teaspoon sugar, 1/2 cup warm water, and 2 1/4 teaspoons yeast. Let sit until foamy, about 10 minutes.
2. Pile 2 1/4 cups (281g) flour on a flat surface and make a well in the middle.
3. Add the yeast mixture, 1/2 cup yogurt, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1 tablespoon oil to the well. Knead until the dough is smooth and shiny, about 10 minutes. If it's sticky, knead in more flour a little at a time.
4. Cover with a damp cloth and let rise in a warm spot (next to the stove or a warm oven) until doubled, about 1 hour.
5. Divide the dough into 8 equal portions and roll each into an 8-inch circle.
6. Heat a skillet (cast iron preferred) over high heat and lightly grease it with oil.
7. Cook one naan at a time: when it puffs up, bubbles, and gets charred spots, flip and cook the other side. Re-grease the skillet as needed.
8. Brush the naan with 3 tablespoons melted butter and serve warm.
