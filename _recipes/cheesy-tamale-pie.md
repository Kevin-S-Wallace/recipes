## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 lb lean ground beef
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 1 can (15 oz) mild red enchilada sauce
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 1 can (4 oz) diced green chiles, undrained
- 1 bag (8 oz) shredded pepper jack
- 2 boxes Jiffy corn muffin mix
- 2 eggs
- 2/3 cup milk
- 1 can (14.75 oz) cream-style corn
- Sour cream or salsa, for serving (optional)

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and spray a 9x13 baking dish.
2. Brown 1 1/2 lb ground beef in a skillet until no pink remains, about 7–8 minutes. Drain if needed.
3. While the beef browns, stir together 2 boxes Jiffy mix, 2 eggs, 2/3 cup milk, and the 14.75 oz can of cream-style corn in a bowl until no dry spots remain.
4. Dump the hot beef into the 9x13. Add 1 packet taco seasoning, the 15 oz can of enchilada sauce, the 15 oz can of drained black beans, 1 cup frozen corn, and the 4 oz can of green chiles with their liquid. Stir right in the dish and spread flat.
5. Sprinkle the 8 oz bag of pepper jack over the top.
6. Pour the cornbread batter over the cheese and spread it to the edges.
7. Bake uncovered at 350°F for about 60 minutes, checking at 50: a toothpick in the center of the cornbread should come out clean.
8. Rest 10 minutes, then scoop. Top with sour cream or salsa if you like.

## Notes
- Don't drain the green chiles; their liquid keeps the filling saucy.
