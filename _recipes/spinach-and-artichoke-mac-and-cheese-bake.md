## Ingredients
- 6 tablespoons salted butter, at room temperature, plus more for greasing
- 1 (1 lb) box short-cut pasta, such as macaroni
- 2 cups whole milk, plus a little extra for thinning
- 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, cubed
- 3 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- Kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
- Ground cayenne pepper
- 2 cups packed fresh baby spinach, chopped
- 1 (8 oz) jar marinated artichokes, drained and roughly chopped
- 1 1/2 cups crushed Ritz crackers (about 1 sleeve)
- 3/4 teaspoon garlic powder

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Grease a 9 x 13-inch baking dish with butter.
2. In a large saucepan, bring 4 cups salted water to a boil over high heat. Add the 1 lb pasta and cook, stirring occasionally, for 8 minutes.
3. Stir in the 2 cups milk and 8 oz cream cheese and cook until the cream cheese has melted and the pasta is al dente, about 5 minutes more. Don't drain.
4. Remove the pan from the heat and stir in 2 cups of the cheddar and 3 tablespoons of the butter. Season with salt, pepper, and cayenne.
5. Stir in the 2 cups spinach and the chopped artichokes. If the sauce feels too thick, add 1/4 cup milk or water to thin it.
6. Transfer the mixture to the prepared baking dish and top with the remaining 1 cup cheddar.
7. In a medium bowl, stir together the 1 1/2 cups crushed crackers, the remaining 3 tablespoons butter, and 3/4 teaspoon garlic powder. Sprinkle the crumbs evenly over the mac and cheese.
8. Bake until the sauce is bubbling and the crumbs are golden, about 20 minutes. Let cool for 5 minutes and serve.

## Notes
- One pot, no draining, no roux: the pasta cooks in just enough water, then finishes in the milk and cream cheese.
- Have a little extra milk or water on hand. Once everything is added, thin the sauce with more liquid if needed.
- Variation: skip the spinach and artichokes, add a few chopped chipotle peppers in adobo, and use half pepper Jack and half cheddar.
- Store leftovers refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
