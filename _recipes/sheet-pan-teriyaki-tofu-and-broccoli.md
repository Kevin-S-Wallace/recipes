## Ingredients
### Tofu
- 2 blocks extra firm tofu
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 4 teaspoons low sodium tamari, or soy sauce
- 4 teaspoons arrowroot starch, or cornstarch

### Broccoli
- 6 cups broccoli florets (or about 3 heads of broccoli)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Kosher salt and fresh ground black pepper, to taste

### Other
- [Teriyaki sauce](teriyaki-sauce.md), to taste
- Brown rice, for serving

## Instructions
1. Drain the tofu and pat dry. For each block, slice into 5 slabs, stack them, slice lengthwise, then slice across into 5 rows (about 50 cubes per block, 100 total).
2. Arrange the cubes in a single layer on a towel-lined cutting board, fold the towel over, and weigh down with something heavy like a cast iron skillet. Press 15-30 minutes, the longer the better.
3. Preheat the oven to 400°F and spray 2 rimmed baking sheets with cooking oil.
4. In a large bowl, toss the tofu with 2 tablespoons olive oil, 4 teaspoons tamari, and 4 teaspoons arrowroot starch until no powdery spots remain.
5. Spread the tofu in an even layer on one baking sheet.
6. In the same bowl, toss the broccoli with 2 tablespoons olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread in an even layer on the second baking sheet.
7. Bake about 24 minutes, tossing halfway and swapping the pans between racks, until the tofu is deeply golden and crisp on the edges.
8. While it bakes, make or warm the [teriyaki sauce](teriyaki-sauce.md).
9. Return the tofu and broccoli to the bowl and toss with as much teriyaki sauce as you like.
10. Serve over brown rice.

## Notes
- Doubled from the original (which serves 4 on one sheet). Use 2 pans, one pan for tofu and one for broccoli
