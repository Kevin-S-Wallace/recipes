## Ingredients
- 1/3 cup low sodium soy sauce (or tamari)
- 1/3 cup water
- 1/3 cup maple syrup or honey
- 1/4 cup rice vinegar
- 1 teaspoon sriracha
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 4 teaspoons cornstarch, mixed with 4 teaspoons water

## Instructions
1. In a small saucepan, whisk together 1/3 cup soy sauce, 1/3 cup water, 1/3 cup maple syrup, 1/4 cup rice vinegar, 1 teaspoon sriracha, 1 teaspoon ground ginger, and 1 teaspoon garlic powder. Bring to a boil.
2. Stir 4 teaspoons cornstarch into 4 teaspoons water until dissolved, then whisk it into the sauce.
3. Lower the heat to a simmer and stir until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, 3-5 minutes.
4. Pour into a jar or airtight container, let cool completely, and refrigerate until ready to use.

## Notes
- Makes about 1 1/4 cups.
