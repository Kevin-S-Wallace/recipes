## Ingredients
- 6 tablespoons low sodium soy sauce (tamari for gluten-free)
- 6 tablespoons water
- 6 tablespoons maple syrup or honey
- 4 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1 teaspoon sriracha
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 4 teaspoons cornstarch, mixed with 4 teaspoons water

## Instructions
1. In a small saucepan, whisk together 6 tablespoons soy sauce, 6 tablespoons water, 6 tablespoons maple syrup, 4 tablespoons rice vinegar, 1 teaspoon sriracha, 1 teaspoon ground ginger, and 1 teaspoon garlic powder. Bring to a boil.
2. Stir 4 teaspoons cornstarch into 4 teaspoons water until dissolved, then whisk it into the sauce.
3. Lower the heat to a simmer and stir until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, 3-5 minutes.
4. Pour into a jar or airtight container, let cool completely, and refrigerate until ready to use.

## Notes
- Makes about 1 1/2 cups.
- Used in [Sheet Pan Teriyaki Tofu and Broccoli](sheet-pan-teriyaki-tofu-and-broccoli.md).
