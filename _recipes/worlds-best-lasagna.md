## Ingredients
### Meat sauce
- 1 lb sweet Italian sausage
- 3/4 lb lean ground beef
- 1/2 cup minced onion
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 2 (6.5 oz) cans tomato sauce
- 2 (6 oz) cans tomato paste
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 tablespoons white sugar
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
- 1 1/2 teaspoons dried basil
- 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon fennel seeds
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper

### Ricotta filling
- 16 oz ricotta cheese
- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

### Assembly
- 12 lasagna noodles
- 3/4 lb mozzarella cheese, sliced
- 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Instructions
1. Cook 1 lb sausage, 3/4 lb ground beef, 1/2 cup minced onion, and 2 crushed garlic cloves in a Dutch oven over medium heat until well browned.
2. Stir in the 28 oz can crushed tomatoes, two 6.5 oz cans tomato sauce, two 6 oz cans tomato paste, and 1/2 cup water. Season with 2 tablespoons sugar, 2 tablespoons parsley, 1 1/2 teaspoons basil, 1 teaspoon salt, 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning, 1/2 teaspoon fennel seeds, and 1/4 teaspoon pepper.
3. Cover and simmer about 1 1/2 hours, stirring occasionally.
4. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook 12 lasagna noodles for 8-10 minutes, then drain and rinse with cold water.
5. In a mixing bowl, combine 16 oz ricotta with 1 egg, the remaining 2 tablespoons parsley, and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
6. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
7. Spread 1 1/2 cups meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Arrange 6 noodles lengthwise over the sauce, overlapping slightly. Spread with half the ricotta mixture, top with 1/3 of the mozzarella slices, spoon 1 1/2 cups meat sauce over the mozzarella, and sprinkle with 1/4 cup Parmesan.
8. Repeat with the remaining 6 noodles, the other half of the ricotta mixture, another 1/3 of the mozzarella, 1 1/2 cups meat sauce, and 1/4 cup Parmesan.
9. Top with the last 1/3 of the mozzarella and the remaining 1/4 cup Parmesan.
10. Cover with foil, either sprayed with cooking spray or tented so it doesn't touch the cheese.
11. Bake 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake 25 minutes more.
12. Let rest about 15 minutes before cutting so it sets and firms up.

## Notes
- For 3 layers instead of 2: use 4 noodles per layer without overlapping, with 1 cup meat sauce and 1/3 of the ricotta mixture in each layer.
