## Ingredients
- 8 tablespoons (120g) butter, divided
- 10 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 cups uncooked long-grain white rice (basmati or jasmine)
- 5 cups chicken broth (or vegetable stock)
- 2 teaspoons dried parsley, plus a little extra to finish
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon fresh cracked black pepper
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped, plus extra to garnish

## Instructions
1. Melt 4 tablespoons butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add 10 minced garlic cloves and stir until fragrant, about 1 minute.
2. Stir in 3 cups rice to coat it in the garlic butter.
3. Add 5 cups broth, 2 teaspoons dried parsley, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon pepper. Stir well and bring to a boil.
4. Cover, reduce the heat to low so it barely simmers, and cook until all the liquid is absorbed, about 15-20 minutes.
5. Uncover and stir in 1/4 cup fresh parsley.
6. Remove from the heat, cover, and let stand 5-10 minutes, until soft.
7. Fluff with a fork. Stir in the remaining 4 tablespoons butter and season with a little extra salt, pepper, and dried parsley.
8. Garnish with more fresh parsley.

## Ingredients
- 4 tablespoons (60g) butter, divided
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 1/2 cups uncooked long-grain white rice (basmati or jasmine)
- 2 1/2 cups chicken broth (or vegetable stock)
- 1 teaspoon dried parsley, plus a little extra to finish
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon fresh cracked black pepper
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped, plus extra to garnish

## Instructions
1. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a medium pot over medium heat. Add 5 minced garlic cloves and stir until fragrant, about 1 minute.
2. Stir in 1 1/2 cups rice to coat it in the garlic butter.
3. Add 2 1/2 cups broth, 1 teaspoon dried parsley, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon pepper. Stir well and bring to a boil.
4. Cover, reduce the heat to low so it barely simmers, and cook until all the liquid is absorbed, about 15 minutes.
5. Uncover and stir in 2 tablespoons fresh parsley.
6. Remove from the heat, cover, and let stand 5-10 minutes, until soft.
7. Fluff with a fork. Stir in the remaining 2 tablespoons butter and season with a little extra salt, pepper, and dried parsley.
8. Garnish with more fresh parsley.
