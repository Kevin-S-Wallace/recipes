## Ingredients
- 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
- 2 tablespoons red curry paste
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- Fresh ginger, grated, to taste
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon peanut butter
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 teaspoons rice vinegar
- Juice of 1 lime
- 4 cups bok choy, chopped
- 15-20 frozen dumplings
- Rice, for serving

### Optional toppings
- Chili crisp
- Green onions
- Sesame seeds
- Lime wedges
- Cilantro

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Start the rice.
2. In a 9x13 pan, whisk together 1 can coconut milk, 2 tablespoons curry paste, 3 minced garlic cloves, the ginger, 3 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 tablespoon oyster sauce, 1 tablespoon peanut butter, 1 tablespoon sesame oil, and 2 teaspoons rice vinegar. Squeeze in the juice of 1 lime.
3. Stir in 4 cups chopped bok choy.
4. Arrange 15-20 frozen dumplings on top in a single layer.
5. Cover with foil and bake 25-30 minutes, until the dumplings are cooked through.
6. Finish with chili crisp, green onions, sesame seeds, lime, and cilantro as desired. Serve hot over rice.
