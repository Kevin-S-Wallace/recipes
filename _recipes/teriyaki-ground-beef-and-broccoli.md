## Ingredients
### Beef and broccoli
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 pound lean ground beef or ground pork
- Salt and fresh ground black pepper, to taste
- 1/4 cup sliced green onions, plus extra for garnish
- Sesame seeds, for garnish
- Rice, for serving

### Teriyaki sauce
- 3 tablespoons low sodium soy sauce (tamari for gluten-free)
- 3 tablespoons water
- 3 tablespoons maple syrup
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1 teaspoon sriracha (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch

## Instructions
1. In a measuring glass, whisk 3 tablespoons soy sauce, 3 tablespoons water, 3 tablespoons maple syrup, 2 tablespoons rice vinegar, 1 teaspoon sriracha, 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger, 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder, and 2 teaspoons cornstarch until the cornstarch dissolves.
2. Heat a skillet over medium-high. Add 2 cups broccoli and 1/4 cup water, cover, and steam 3 minutes, until bright green and crisp-tender. Move the broccoli to a plate.
3. Spray the skillet with cooking oil and add 1 pound ground beef. Season with salt and pepper and break it into crumbles. Add 1/4 cup green onions.
4. Once the meat is cooked through, return the broccoli to the skillet and pour in the sauce. Stir until the sauce thickens, about 3 minutes.
5. Taste for seasoning. Top with sesame seeds and extra green onions and serve over rice.

## Notes
- The sauce here is close to a half batch of [Teriyaki Sauce](teriyaki-sauce.md), made straight in the measuring cup.
