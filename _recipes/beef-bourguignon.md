## Ingredients
- 2 lb beef chuck, fat trimmed away, cubed
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- Kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
- 4 slices thick-cut bacon, chopped
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced or grated
- 4 carrots, sliced
- 1 cup sliced cremini mushrooms
- 2 cups dry red wine, such as Pinot Noir
- 1 cup low-sodium beef broth
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- Mashed potatoes, for serving

## Instructions

### Dutch oven
1. Heat the oven to 325°F.
2. In a large bowl, toss 2 lb cubed beef chuck with 2 tablespoons flour and a pinch each of salt and pepper to coat.
3. In a Dutch oven or heavy lidded pot over medium heat, cook 4 slices chopped bacon, stirring often, until the fat renders and the bacon is crispy, about 5 minutes. Drain the bacon on a paper towel-lined plate and discard all but 1 tablespoon of the grease.
4. Raise the heat to medium-high and brown the beef on all sides in 2 batches so the pot isn't crowded, about 5 minutes per batch. Move the beef to a plate.
5. Lower the heat to medium. Add 1 diced onion, 4 sliced carrots, 1 cup sliced mushrooms, and a pinch each of salt and pepper and cook until the onion softens, about 5 minutes.
6. Stir in 2 minced garlic cloves and 2 tablespoons tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.
7. Pour in 2 cups red wine, scraping up the browned bits, then add 1 cup beef broth, 4 thyme sprigs, the bacon, and the beef with its juices. Bring to a simmer.
8. Cover and braise in the oven until the beef is fork-tender, 2 1/2 to 3 hours.
9. Remove and discard the thyme sprigs. If the sauce is thin, simmer it uncovered on the stove until it thickens slightly, about 10 minutes.
10. Serve the beef over mashed potatoes.

### Slow cooker
1. In a large bowl, toss 2 lb cubed beef chuck with 2 tablespoons flour and a pinch each of salt and pepper to coat.
2. In a large skillet over medium heat, cook 4 slices chopped bacon, stirring often, until the fat renders and the bacon is crispy, about 5 minutes. Drain the bacon on a paper towel-lined plate and discard all but 1 tablespoon of the grease.
3. Raise the heat to medium-high and brown the beef on all sides in 2 batches, about 5 minutes per batch. Move the beef to the slow cooker.
4. Lower the heat to medium. Stir 2 minced garlic cloves and 2 tablespoons tomato paste into the skillet and cook for 1 minute, then pour in 2 cups red wine and scrape up the browned bits. Pour it all into the slow cooker.
5. Add 1 diced onion, 4 sliced carrots, 1 cup sliced mushrooms, 1 cup beef broth, 4 thyme sprigs, the bacon, and a pinch each of salt and pepper. Stir to combine.
6. Cover and cook on low for 7 to 8 hours or on high for 4 to 5 hours, until the beef is fork-tender.
7. Remove and discard the thyme sprigs. The sauce will be thinner than the other methods. To thicken it, cook uncovered on high for 30 minutes, or pour the liquid into a saucepan and simmer until it reduces slightly, about 10 minutes.
8. Serve the beef over mashed potatoes.

### Pressure cooker
1. In a large bowl, toss 2 lb cubed beef chuck with 2 tablespoons flour and a pinch each of salt and pepper to coat.
2. Scatter 4 slices chopped bacon into the pressure cooker pot and, on sauté, cook, stirring often, until the fat renders and the bacon is crispy, about 5 minutes. Drain the bacon on a paper towel-lined plate and discard all but 1 tablespoon of the grease.
3. Add the beef to the pot and, on sauté, brown on all sides, about 5 minutes.
4. Stir in 1 diced onion, 2 minced garlic cloves, 4 sliced carrots, 1 cup sliced mushrooms, and a pinch each of salt and pepper.
5. Pour in 2 cups red wine and 1 cup beef broth, then add 2 tablespoons tomato paste, 4 thyme sprigs, and the bacon. Stir to combine.
6. Lock the lid, select high pressure, and cook for 35 minutes.
7. Quick or natural release, then open when the pressure subsides. Remove and discard the thyme sprigs.
8. On sauté, simmer until the sauce thickens slightly, about 10 minutes.
9. Serve the beef over mashed potatoes.

## Notes
- From the Half Baked Harvest cookbook (Beef & Lamb chapter, p. 209). The book's version is the pressure cooker method; the Dutch oven and slow cooker methods are adaptations.
- Total time by method: Dutch oven about 3 hr 30 min, slow cooker 5 to 9 hr (mostly hands-off), pressure cooker about 1 hr 10 min plus time to come to pressure.
- The book pairs it with buttery mashed potatoes and crusty no-knead bread; store-bought mashed potatoes work too.
- Store leftovers refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
